Healthcare software has a very different set of constraints from most consumer products. Here are three lessons that stuck with me.

## 1. Talk to the people doing the work

The best requirements came from sitting with nurses and administrators, not from spec documents. Small details, like how many clicks a task takes during a busy shift, mattered more than any feature list.

## 2. Boring technology is a feature

Reliability beats novelty. We picked well-understood tools so the team could focus on the problem instead of the stack.

## 3. Ship small, verify often

> Every release should be small enough to explain in one sentence.

Short feedback loops made it much easier to catch mistakes before they reached users.
